Domain 1

Security, Compliance, and Identity Concepts

Understand the foundational concepts used across the rest of SC-900.

Shared Responsibility

Microsoft and the customer share security responsibilities depending on the cloud model. Customers always own data, endpoint devices, accounts, and access management.

Zero Trust

Verify explicitly, use least privilege, and assume breach. Zero Trust is a strategy, not a single product.

Defense in Depth

Use multiple layers of protection: physical, identity, perimeter, network, compute, application, and data.

Exam Tip

Authentication verifies who you are. Authorization decides what you can access.
